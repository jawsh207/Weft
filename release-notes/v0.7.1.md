**Weft 0.7.1 — "i" is always "I"**

### Fixed
- "i", "i'm", "i'll", "i've" and "i'd" now always become "I", "I'm", "I'll", "I've" and "I'd", in every text field except passwords, emails and web addresses, even where autocorrect is off
- Pressing delete right after "I" undoes it only that once; it no longer makes Weft leave "i" lowercase for good

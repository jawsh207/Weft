**Weft 0.7.0 — see and edit what Weft has learned**

### New
- Settings › View and edit opens a Learned words screen with four tabs: new words, word pairs, words kept as typed, and suggestions you removed
- Edit, delete or add words and pairs; let autocorrect fix a word again, or bring back a removed suggestion
- Search once a list gets long

### Improved
- Changes apply to the keyboard straight away, even while it's open, and are never undone when the keyboard saves; Clear works the same way
- Respelling a word keeps its usage and word pairs

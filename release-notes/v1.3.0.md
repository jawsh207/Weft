**Weft 1.3.0 — smarter next-word predictions**

### New
- Next-word predictions now read the whole sentence so far, not just the last word or two, using a small language model built into the keyboard. On the benchmark, the right next word shows up in the top three 44.8% of the time, up from 36.8%, and 38.3% in casual conversation, up from 28.3%
- The model runs entirely on your phone, with no internet access and no extra libraries, and only kicks in between words: corrections and autocorrect work as before
- Settings › About lists the openly licensed text the model learned from

### Improved
- Predictions save a little more typing: 48.8% of key presses on the benchmark, up from 46.5%
- The app is about 6 MB larger to make room for the model

**Weft 0.8.0 — swipe that learns your thumb**

### New
- Swipe learns from you, on this phone only: where your thumb tends to land compared with the letters you mean, and which look-alike word you pick ("four" over "for"). It adjusts after a few dozen swipes and keeps following if your grip changes. Swipes you delete teach it nothing, and Clear in settings wipes it
- A bubble above your finger shows the best guess while you swipe
- Letters light up as your swipe crosses them

### Improved
- The swipe trail tapers and fades toward its tail
- New setting: Swipe trail and preview, to turn the trail, lit letters and bubble off

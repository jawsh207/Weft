**Weft 0.9.0 — tidier settings**

### Improved
- Settings is now a short main page with sections, each on its own page: Typing, Swipe typing, Learning, Keys and feel, Voice input, and About. Setup steps show only until they're done
- Voice input settings have a shortcut to choose your phone's speech engine
- With Swipe trail and preview off, swiping shows the plain trail again

### Fixed
- Swiping with the trail and preview on no longer stretches the keyboard to fill the screen

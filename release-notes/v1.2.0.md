**Weft 1.2.0 — key sounds**

### New
- Settings › Keys and feel › Sound on key press: Off, Quiet, Normal or Loud. It uses Android's own keyboard clicks, with backspace, space and enter each sounding a little different
- Sounds are off until you turn them on, and stay quiet when the phone is on silent or vibrate
- Choosing a level plays it once, so you can hear it before you type

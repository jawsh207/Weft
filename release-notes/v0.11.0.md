**Weft 0.11.0 — swiping reads your rhythm**

### New
- Swipe typing now notices where your finger slows down, and uses it to choose between words that trace nearly the same path. Pausing on a letter makes it count: along the straight line from p to t, pausing on "o" gives "pot" rather than "put"

### Improved
- Swipes are recorded in finer detail, using every touch point the screen reports
- The Swipe settings summary now matches the "Enhanced visuals" name

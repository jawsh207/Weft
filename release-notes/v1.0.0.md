**Weft 1.0.0 — now under the Apache 2.0 license**

### New
- Weft's code is licensed under Apache 2.0 from this release on. Earlier releases (up to 0.11.0) stay available under GPL-3.0
- The dictionaries are rebuilt without the subtitle word list, the one data source whose share-alike terms didn't fit. Everyday word rankings now come from open sentence collections, and every source is credited in Settings › About and in the NOTICE file

### Improved
- Texting words such as "btw", "lol", "omg", "idk" and "thx" are now suggested readily
- Newer words are known: "COVID", "coronavirus", "blockchain", "cryptocurrency"
- On our tests, typing predictions are as good or slightly better than before, and swipe accuracy is about half a point lower

### Install note
- About 2,600 rarely used words changed, mostly film and TV character names leaving. If a word you use stops being suggested, typing it and tapping it in the suggestion bar teaches it to the keyboard

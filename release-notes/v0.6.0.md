**Weft 0.6.0 — voice input that fits your sentence**

### New
- Punctuation goes in only when you say it: "period" (or "full stop"), "comma", "question mark", "exclamation point", "colon", "semicolon" and "new line". The words stay words when you mean them ("the period of time"), and saying one twice types the word
- Voice input no longer ends with punctuation you didn't ask for

### Improved
- Capitals fit where your cursor is: the first spoken word is capitalized at the start of a sentence and lowercase in the middle of one, while "I", names, places and days keep their capitals
- Works the same with any speech engine, including ones that return plain lowercase text
- The live preview no longer flickers a period while you speak, with engines that support it
- Search boxes and address bars ask your speech engine for search-style recognition

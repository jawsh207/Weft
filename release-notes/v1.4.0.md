**Weft 1.4.0 — predictions from the first word, a smaller app**

### New
- Predictions at the start of a sentence or phrase: in an empty field, after a line break, and after . ! ? , ; : the strip now suggests the first word (capitalized while shift is on), where before it was blank
- Swiping adds the space as soon as you lift your finger, so next-word predictions show right away. Delete once to see the swipe's other guesses, twice to remove the word

### Improved
- The app is about 9 MB smaller: the US, UK, Canadian and Australian English dictionaries now share one base, each keeping only its own spellings and words
- A better-trained next-word model: the right next word is in the top three 45.2% of the time on the benchmark (up from 44.8%) and 39.3% in conversation (up from 38.3%). Predictions save 49.5% of key presses, up from 48.8%
- The model no longer suggests names like "Tom" on its own

**Weft 0.4.0 — make it yours**

### New
- Keys and voice settings, which apply right away, even while the keyboard is open:
  - Vibration on key press: Off, Light, Normal or Strong
  - Key height: Compact, Normal or Tall. Swipe typing works the same at every height
  - Number row: show 1–0 above the letters
  - Prefer offline voice: asks your chosen speech engine to recognize speech on the phone when it can. Weft always uses the engine chosen in system settings

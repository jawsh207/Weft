**Weft 0.3.0 — learns how you type**

### New
- Weft learns from your typing, on this device only: the words you use most and which words you type together. Suggestions, next-word predictions and swipe all rank by your habits
- Next-word predictions from your own word pairs, including after names and other words the dictionary doesn't know
- Long-press a suggestion and tap Remove to stop Weft suggesting it, in typing and in swipe

### Improved
- Undoing an autocorrect now keeps that word as you typed it for good, not just until the keyboard restarts
- Settings shows how many new words and word pairs Weft has learned, and Clear wipes everything at once, even while the keyboard is open
- Nothing is learned in email or web address fields, as well as password and incognito fields

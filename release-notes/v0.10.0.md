**Weft 0.10.0 — a much smaller download**

### Improved
- The app is about 13 MB instead of 44 MB. The dictionaries use a more compact format and are unpacked the first time they're needed, with the same accuracy as before
- Swiping is cleaner: the bubble above your finger is gone, since the middle suggestion already shows the best guess
- The swipe trail setting is now called "Enhanced visuals"

### Install note
- After updating, the first time a language loads may take a moment while its dictionary is unpacked

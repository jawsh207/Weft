**Weft 0.5.0 — a smarter dictionary**

### New
- English for the UK, Canada and Australia: pick the keyboard language in your phone's settings, and Weft uses that region's spellings (colour, organise, centre, tyre, mum)
- Predictions look two words back: "see you" suggests "later", "I want" suggests "to"
- Names and places with their capitals: thousands of first names and surnames, cities, countries, nationalities and brands ("Seattle", "Priya", "Spotify")
- Emoji suggestions: typing "pizza" offers 🍕 and "lol" offers 😂, in your usual skin tone. Tap one to add it after the word; turn them off in settings

### Improved
- Autocorrect fixes about twice as many misspellings, including rarer words ("Farenheit" → "Fahrenheit"), and still never changes real words
- A one-letter fix beats splitting a word in two: "allthough" becomes "although", while "letgo" still becomes "let go"
- Names you type are left alone: a capitalized word in the middle of a sentence is never changed into another word
- Fast, sloppy swipes are recognized far more often
- More everyday word pairs from Tatoeba and Mozilla Common Voice

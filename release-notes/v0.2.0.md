**Weft 0.2.0 — tools key and steadier suggestions**

### New
- Tools key on the left of the suggestion strip: tap it to slide out Paste, Clear clipboard and Settings over the suggestions. Paste and Clear are greyed out when the clipboard is empty

### Fixed
- Tapped words now get suggestions and spelling corrections in every text field where swipe works. Some apps and websites ask keyboards not to suggest; there Weft still shows suggestions and corrections in the strip, but won't change a word on its own when you press space
